Privacy notice

This notice explains what we collect when you join MOVA Miles, our loyalty card, and what we do with it. MOVA Coffee is run by [legal company name, company number], Unit 10, High Cross Centre, Fountain Road, London N15 4QM. We are responsible for your details. Contact us at movacoffeehouse@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your first name and email address, when you join.
  • Each time your card is scanned: the date and time, how many coffees, and which member of staff stamped it. We use the time to work out your MOVA moment (for example, Early Mover).
  • When you use a free coffee.
  • Whether you've said yes to marketing emails.

We don't collect payment details through MOVA Miles, and we don't track you online.

Why we use it

  • To run your loyalty card: counting stamps, giving you free coffees and showing your history. We need your details to provide the card you signed up for.
  • To understand our shop: for example, how busy the morning commute is. We look at this in totals.
  • To email you about new coffees, events and offers, only if you ticked the box. You can untick it in your card's settings at any time, or use the unsubscribe link in any email.

Who else handles it

Our loyalty system runs on Supabase, which stores the data for us on servers in [the UK / EU region you choose]. Sign-in emails are sent through [email provider]. These companies only handle your details on our instructions. We never sell your details.

How long we keep it

We keep your card while you're a member. If you delete your account, your details and history are deleted straight away. If a card hasn't been used for [24 months], we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your details, ask us to correct them, or delete your account yourself from your card's settings. Email us for anything else. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

MOVA Miles keeps you signed in using your browser's storage on your own phone. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
