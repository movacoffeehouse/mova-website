Four stops on your day.

Whatever time you come in, there's something made for that moment. Every visit also counts towards your MOVA moment on MOVA Miles.

The sandwiches

Made fresh every morning.

Our own recipes on ciabatta, focaccia and sourdough. Ask for any of them hot-pressed.

California Club

Chicken, avocado, lettuce, garlic aioli and bacon on ciabatta.

Chicken Kiev

Chicken kiev, garlic butter sauce, homemade pesto, rocket and parmesan on focaccia.

The Aberdeen

Aberdeenshire salt beef, peppery rocket and organic honey mustard in thick toasted ciabatta.

The Nordic

Scottish smoked salmon, cream cheese, cucumber and fresh dill on warm ciabatta.

Chargrilled halloumi, smashed avocado, roasted red peppers and sweet chilli glaze on toasted sourdough.
